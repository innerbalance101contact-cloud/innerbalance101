Voice.

Voice is not written down on this page. It lives in one place: the ib101-founder-voice skill. Every piece of copy, for every platform and for the website, is drafted by calling that skill. When the voice changes, the skill changes, and nothing here has to be edited.

  • The skill is the source of truth for how things are said. If any document disagrees with it, the skill wins.
  • Documents hold what gets said. Positioning, facts, product and pricing stay in the master brand document and the Notion Brand Book.
  • Before publishing, copy also goes through the humanizer skill and a claims check for absolute promises.

Type.

Vollkorn for headlines and quotes. Jost for body and interface. Lining numerals everywhere. Base size 18px.

Vollkorn 500

Headlines, pull quotes, prices and the big numbers.

Tight tracking on large sizes, balanced line breaks.
Jost 400

Body, navigation, labels and buttons. Medium 500 for emphasis. Line length stays under about 62 characters.

Never all-caps body copy. Never bold sans headlines.

Rules

  • One clay button style across the whole site. The main call to action is always “Take the free assessment”.
  • No eyebrows. No small uppercase labels above headings.
  • No italic accent words inside headlines.
  • No arrows on buttons.
  • No three-equal-card rows as the default layout. Use asymmetric 5 and 7 column splits, lists and rules instead.
  • Dusty Rose once per page and only a little.

Logo

  • The Equilibrium Mark: a circle with a horizontal balance line, two clay dots and a dashed vertical axis.
  • Never stretch it, recolour it or add effects. Keep clear space equal to the circle radius. Never smaller than 32px.
  • Use it on Paper or on Forest or Bark. Not on busy photos.

Photography.

Warm natural light, candles, a diffuser, a wooden desk, real settings. No yoga mats, no lotus poses, no stock wellness imagery.

  • Grade: Pine Shadow. Shadows pulled to Pine #4F6861, mid-tones to Clay Light #DDD0BE, highlights to Paper #FAF7F2. 42% blend, 30% desaturation, lifted blacks, light film grain. Skin tones stay natural.
  • Grade the original once. Never regrade a graded file. Keep originals.
  • Logos, diagrams and illustrations are not graded.
  • Every image carries a plain-language alt text. Decorative images are marked as decorative.

Accessibility.

Every redesigned page is built to these rules. They are checkable, not a matter of taste.

  • Contrast. All text meets WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1 or better. See the table above.
  • Keyboard. Every control can be reached and used with the keyboard. A skip link jumps past the navigation.
  • Focus. A visible 3px focus ring on every interactive element. Pine on light grounds, Clay on dark grounds.
  • Touch targets. At least 44px high. The main button is 54px.
  • Structure. One h1 per page, headings in order, landmarks for navigation, main content and footer.
  • Motion. Smooth scrolling is off for people who ask for reduced motion. The hero before and after loops by design and is the only looping motion on the site.
  • Colour is never the only signal. State changes also use text, shape or position.
  • Language. Plain words, short sentences, real quotation marks and apostrophes, and non-breaking spaces so a number never separates from its unit.
  • Links. External links open with rel=“noopener noreferrer”. Link text says where it goes.

Checked with an automated audit (axe-core) plus a manual pass on each redesigned page. Known gap: pages restyled in a later pass (login, dashboard, admin, resources, the sales pages, privacy, terms and the gift page) use the same tokens but have not been through the full audit yet.

Responsive.

Designed for a phone first, then widened. No horizontal scrolling at any width.

  • Breakpoints. 960px, where two-column layouts stack into one. 640px, where the navigation becomes a menu button and buttons go full width.
  • Fluid type. Headlines scale with the screen using clamp, so they never overflow or shrink to nothing.
  • Fluid spacing. Section padding and the side gutter (20px on a phone, up to 64px on a desktop) scale with the screen.
  • Images never exceed their container. Tables and wide content are rebuilt as lists on small screens.
  • Tested at 320px and 390px phone widths and a 1440px desktop before a page goes live.

The design skills behind this version.

Three skills were run across the 13 redesigned pages. Use the same three for any new page.

Design taste

Keeps the site from looking like a template. It decides composition and restraint.

  • Asymmetric layouts and generous space instead of rows of identical cards
  • One button, one accent colour for action, one rose moment
  • Headlines that carry the page, with no eyebrows or decorative accents

Vercel web interface guidelines

Interface craft and correctness.

  • Visible focus states, 44px touch targets and touch-action set for faster taps
  • Balanced and pretty line breaks, lining and tabular numerals, non-breaking spaces
  • Real quotation marks, safe external links, no layout shift, reduced motion respected

TypeUI clean

A clean, consistent system with accessibility built in.

  • One set of colour, type and spacing tokens shared by every page
  • Accessible components: skip link, landmarks, readable contrast, proper headings
  • Same navigation, footer and button on every page

New pages are built from the shared template, then run through a polish pass and an audit before they are published.

Who does what.

innerbalance101 is run by Naomi and AI. There is no virtual assistant and no hand-off team.

Naomi decides

  • All first-person founder content
  • New products, offers and pricing
  • Anything that tells her personal story
  • Replies to press, partners and sensitive messages
  • Financial decisions and what goes live
  • Posting to social platforms

AI does

  • Drafting copy and content in the brand voice for Naomi to approve
  • Designing and building site pages and updates
  • Preparing graphics, descriptions and templates
  • Reporting, checks and audits
  • Keeping Notion and the brand documents current

Check with Naomi first: any new tool, any change to pricing, any new platform, and anything that represents the brand in a new context.
