Plain language summary: We collect your name and email when you sign up for our email list or take our assessment. We use it to send you content and product information. We do not sell your data. You can unsubscribe or request deletion at any time.

1. Who We Are

innerbalance101 ("we", "our", or "us") is a wellness education brand operated by Naomi Pereira, based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. We operate the website at innerbalance101.com and associated digital products including the Emotional Load Assessment and The Inner Balance System.

We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. For questions or requests, contact us at innerbalance101.contact@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name and email address — when you subscribe to our email list, take the Emotional Load Assessment, or submit a contact form.
  • Assessment responses — when you complete our Emotional Load Assessment, we collect your answers and the resulting scores (awareness score, load score, and dimension scores). This information is used to personalise your results and email follow-up.
  • Purchase information — if you purchase The Inner Balance System or other products, transaction data is processed by our third-party payment and product delivery platforms (see Section 6).
  • Messages — if you email us or contact us through our site, we retain the content of those communications.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on page, referral source, and browser or device type, collected via Google Analytics.
  • IP address — collected automatically by our web hosting infrastructure.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see Section 5 for details.

Information from advertising platforms

If you interact with our advertisements on LinkedIn, Facebook, or Instagram, those platforms may share limited data with us, such as your name and email address via a Lead Gen Form, or conversion data when you take an action on our site after clicking an ad. This data is governed both by this policy and the relevant platform's own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To deliver the Emotional Load Assessment results and personalised follow-up content based on your quadrant profile.
  • To send you email content, product information, and offers via our email platform (Kit, formerly ConvertKit). You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • To process purchases and deliver digital products.
  • To improve our website and content based on aggregated usage data.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising campaigns and understand how visitors find us.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data to make automated decisions that produce significant legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Advertising and LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms

This section specifically addresses LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms as required by LinkedIn's advertising policies. By submitting a Lead Gen Form on LinkedIn, you consent to innerbalance101 receiving and using your submitted information as described here.

LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms

We may run advertisements on LinkedIn that include Lead Gen Forms. When you submit a Lead Gen Form, LinkedIn transmits your pre-filled profile information (typically your name and email address) to us. By submitting that form, you agree to:

  • Having your name and email address received by innerbalance101.
  • Being added to our email list and receiving our content and product communications.
  • Your data being processed as described in this privacy policy.

You may unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. To request deletion of your data, contact us at innerbalance101.contact@gmail.com.

LinkedIn Advertising generally

We may use LinkedIn's advertising tools including Matched Audiences, conversion tracking, and the LinkedIn Insight Tag. These tools allow us to measure ad performance and reach relevant audiences. LinkedIn's use of this data is governed by LinkedIn's Privacy Policy.

Facebook and Instagram Ads

We may run ads on Facebook and Instagram using Meta's advertising platform, including the Meta Pixel for conversion tracking and Custom Audiences. Meta's use of this data is governed by Meta's Data Policy.

Google Ads

We may use Google Ads for search and display advertising, including remarketing. Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ad Settings.

Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, our legal basis for processing data collected via advertising is consent, which you provide when submitting a Lead Gen Form or accepting cookies on our website. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. A cookie is a small file placed on your device that helps us recognise you and understand how you use our site.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. These cookies collect anonymised data about pages visited and time spent. You can opt out at Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • Advertising cookies — if you have interacted with our ads, the LinkedIn Insight Tag, Meta Pixel, or Google Ads tracking code may place cookies to measure conversions and enable remarketing.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of our site. For more information on managing cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your personal data. Each is bound by its own privacy policy:

Email marketing

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — we use Kit to manage our email list, send emails, and run automation sequences. Kit processes your name and email address on our behalf. Kit Privacy Policy.

Product delivery

Beacons — our digital products are delivered via Beacons. When you purchase, Beacons processes payment and delivery information. Beacons Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Advertising platforms

LinkedIn, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads — see Section 4 for details. Each platform has its own data policy governing how they use data associated with ad delivery and performance measurement.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers — we share data with the third-party platforms listed in Section 6, solely for the purposes described in this policy.
  • For legal compliance — we may disclose data if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfers — if innerbalance101 is acquired or merged with another entity, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email if this occurs.
  • With your consent — we may share data for other purposes if you give us explicit consent to do so.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as needed to provide our services and fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

  • Email subscribers — we retain your data while you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we delete your contact record within 30 days, unless you have made purchases that require us to retain records for legal or tax purposes.
  • Assessment data — retained for the duration of your subscription relationship with us.
  • Purchase records — retained for seven years in accordance with Canadian tax and accounting requirements.
  • Analytics data — Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default.

You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 9).

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. We honour these rights for all users regardless of jurisdiction.

Rights available to all users

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Opt out of marketing — unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any email, or by contacting us directly.
  • Data portability — request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.

Additional rights for EEA / UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Restriction of processing — request that we restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Object to processing — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Rights for Canadian residents (PIPEDA)

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to access your personal information and challenge its accuracy. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.

Rights for California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, to opt out of sale (we do not sell personal information), and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at innerbalance101.contact@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

innerbalance101 is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a person under 18, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we may have collected data from a minor, please contact us at innerbalance101.contact@gmail.com.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Our website is served over HTTPS. Our email platform (Kit) and product platform (Beacons) maintain their own security standards.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we do our best to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify active email subscribers.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website or services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
